CATALOG & SPECIFICATIONS

Engineered for Stylists, Salons & Global Wholesalers

100% ethically sourced Indian raw hair, hygienically cleaned, cuticle-aligned, and graded for international export.

Voluminous & Dense

Non - Remy Double Drawn

Collected from various sources and undergoes processing to align the strands. Shorter hairs are removed to ensure a thick and full appearance from root to tip.

  • Provides a voluminous and uniform look with evenly cut strands.
  • More affordable than Remy hair while still offering a dense and polished finish.
  • Chemically treated to reduce tangling and improve longevity.
Single Donor 100% Pure

Virgin Remy Single Drawn

Sourced from a single donor, ensuring all cuticles are intact and aligned in one direction for a natural, tangle-free experience.

  • Naturally tapered ends give a lightweight, realistic appearance.
  • Perfect for a natural, effortless style with an authentic texture.
  • High-quality hair at a more affordable price, ideal for everyday use.
Machine & Hand-Tied

Weft

Wefts are created by sewing or bonding hair strands together in a band, making them easy to use for wigs or extensions.

  • Machine wefted for durability or hand-tied for a lightweight, natural feel.
  • Versatile for sewing, gluing, or clipping into hair.
  • Available in various textures and colors to suit every need.
Clip-Ins & Tape-Ins

Extensions

Extensions are add-ons made from natural human hair to add length, volume, or both to natural hair.

  • Clip-ins, tape-ins, or sew-ins for easy application.
  • Provides instant length and volume for a customizable look.
  • Perfect for styling versatility without damaging natural hair.
Full Head Transformation

Wigs

Wigs are crafted from natural human hair and designed to cover the entire head, offering complete style transformation.

  • Available in various styles, lengths, and textures.
  • Protective styling that safeguards natural hair while providing versatility.
  • Suitable for everyday use or special occasions with natural-looking results.
