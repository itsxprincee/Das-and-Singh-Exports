AGRICULTURAL EXPORT • BIHAR ORIGIN

Makhana (Fox Nuts / Lotus Seeds)

Makhana, also known as fox nuts, lotus seeds, gorgon nuts, or phool makhana, is a premium, naturally gluten-free, high-nutrient seed derived from the aquatic plant Euryale ferox. Traditionally cultivated in freshwater ponds of Bihar, India, it is globally valued for its light texture, long shelf life, and exceptional health benefits.
